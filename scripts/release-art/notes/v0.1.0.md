## What's Changed

- A first board: tickets in columns, drag a card from one stage to the next
- Tickets have a title, instructions and a feed of posts
- Agents connect with a token and post progress on a ticket
- Sign in with a passkey on the machine that runs the board
- Attach screenshots to a post
- A first MCP server: list, read and claim tickets
