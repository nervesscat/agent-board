# Telugu (te) glossary

Language: Telugu (తెలుగు script). Register: polite మీరు, imperative for buttons and short verbs, one form everywhere.
Numbers stay Latin digits as in the English; the app formats dates and numbers itself.

Technical words developers say in English are kept in Latin letters and never inflected; a Telugu case ending is
attached directly (`ticketను`, `repoలలో`, `branchలు`) as Telugu developers write it. Plural of a kept word uses a
Telugu ending (`tickets` is kept as the English plural in counts: "{{count}} tickets").

Plural forms: `_one` + `_other` (Telugu `Intl.PluralRules`: one for 1, other for the rest). Keys without a count
differ only where the English does.

Unsure about: "మీ కోసం" for Needs you (short, may read as "for you" out of context); "సంప్రదింపు తెగింది" for Lost
contact; "నిశ్శబ్దం" for Quiet; palette keywords mix Telugu and English on purpose so either searches.
Values identical to English (76) are kept terms, brand/product names, code, or placeholder-only strings.
