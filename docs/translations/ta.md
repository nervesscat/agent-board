# Tamil (ta) glossary

Language: Tamil (தமிழ்). Register: polite-neutral. Buttons, menu items and labels are the bare imperative stem
or a noun (சேமி, நீக்கு, திற); sentences that tell the user to act end in "-வும்" (செய்யவும்).
Latin digits as in the English text. Punctuation as in English.

Kept in Latin letters, never inflected (Tamil words follow without a case suffix, or the sentence is
restructured; a hyphen plus suffix such as "ticket-ஐ" is used where a case ending is unavoidable):
ticket, lane, PR, preview, commit, branch, merge, worktree, workspace, checkout, token, webhook, CLI, skill,
passkey, tailnet, Funnel, prompt, MCP, repo, push, build, builder/spec/runner, backlog, CI, heartbeat.

Plural forms: `_one` + `_other` (Intl.PluralRules("ta")). Tamil nouns after a number do not change, so most
pairs differ only by the "-கள்" ending of kept English words.

Unsure: the long help texts in Settings and Cleanup were written for meaning first and may run long on a
390 px width; "Archived" (a page name) is sometimes kept in Latin; the 77 values equal to English are brand,
code, placeholders only or kept terms (ticket, lane, PR, Previews, Branch, Worktree, Prompt, Tailscale ...).
