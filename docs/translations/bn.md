# Bengali (bn) glossary

Standard Bengali (Bangladesh and West Bengal), Bangla script, Latin digits as in the English text.

Register: polite neutral imperative (করুন / -ুন) in sentences and tooltips; buttons are short verb stems or
nouns (সংরক্ষণ, বাতিল, মুছুন). Sentences end with "।"; English technical terms stay in Latin letters with a
hyphenated Bengali suffix (ticket-এর, repo-তে, PR-গুলো).

Plural forms: `_one` + `_other` (Bengali `one` covers 0 and 1, so `{{count}}` is kept in both).
Countable nouns take "-টি" / "জন" (people and agents) after the number.
