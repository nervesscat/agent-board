# Hindi (hi): glossary of decisions

Register: आप (polite). Buttons and tooltips use the imperative in the polite form ("सहेजें", "खोलें",
"कॉपी करें"). Sentence-style texts follow the English sentence type. Hindi punctuation: the danda "।" ends
sentences where the English has a period. Short wording is preferred (Hindi runs longer than English).

Kept in English (Latin letters), as Indian developers say them: ticket, lane, PR, preview, commit, branch,
merge, rebase, push, worktree, workspace, checkout, token, webhook, CLI, skill, passkey, tailnet, Funnel,
prompt, builder / spec / runner / env / verify / hunt / refute / intent, backlog, hand-off, origin, SHA,
MCP, and the brand CrossBoard. They are never inflected: the Hindi postposition follows them ("ticket का", "branch में").

Plural forms: `_one` and `_other` (Hindi `one` covers 0 and 1, so `{{count}}` stays in both). Most nouns are
the same in both forms; verbs and adjectives that change ("रुका / रुके", "नया / नए") are split.

Unsure about:

- "आपकी बारी" for "Needs you" (short and idiomatic, but looser than "आपकी ज़रूरत").
- "चरण" for stage (could be "स्टेज"); "फ़ेज़" for the default phases.
- The palette keywords mix Hindi and English words on purpose, so both kinds of search match.
