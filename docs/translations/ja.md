# Japanese (ja) glossary

Register: です/ます in sentences; buttons and labels in noun form or plain verb (保存, 削除, 再試行), as Japanese
software does. Full-width punctuation (。、「」), half-width brackets and colons kept like the English; no spaces
around `{{placeholders}}` except where a Latin token needs air. Developer terms are written in katakana, the
way Japanese developers say them; only PR, CLI, MCP, CI and Latin names/commands stay in Latin letters.

| preview, commit, branch, merge | プレビュー, コミット, ブランチ, マージ                     |

| token, passkey, webhook, skill | トークン, パスキー, ウェブフック, スキル                   |

Plural forms: only `_other` (Japanese has one plural category). The desktop table likewise.

Unsure: "Reach" column in Cleanup (反映), "Quiet" (動きなし), "Needs" as a dependency (依存), the fixed
sentence-ending words in generated timeline stories (their notes come from the app and may read slightly stiff),
and the date-only phrases like "{{date}}の週". Values identical to English are 15: Tailscale, HTTPS, Funnel, git,
brand/version, "PR #{{number}}", sizes, and strings made only of placeholders.
