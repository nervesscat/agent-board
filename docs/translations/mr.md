# Marathi (mr) glossary

Marathi (मराठी, Devanagari), not Hindi: Marathi grammar and vocabulary (आहे, करा, तुम्ही). Register: तुम्ही,
buttons and tooltips in the imperative plural-polite form (उघडा, जतन करा). Latin digits as in the English; the
app formats numbers and dates itself.

Plural forms: `_one` + `_other` (as English). Most nouns are invariant in the count phrase ("{{count}} ticket").

Kept in English (Latin letters, no suffix attached; a space then the postposition: "ticket चा"): ticket, lane, PR,
preview, job, commit, branch, merge, rebase, worktree, workspace, checkout, token, webhook, CLI, skill, passkey,
tailnet, Funnel, prompt, push, fetch, origin, builder/spec/runner, backlog, heartbeat, span, MCP.
