The ticket agent's own self-check, before the human reads the draft. It runs no build and no test suite: it reads the draft itself against the ticket.

1. Read the whole draft against the ticket's own words, criterion by criterion: each thing the ticket asks for is in it, and in the form it asks for.
2. Check every name, figure, date and link in the draft against the ticket's words and attachments. Anything that the ticket does not give is removed or put back to the human as a question; it is never kept as a guess.
3. Check the draft for what it must not say: a claim the human did not make, a promise the ticket does not make, and wording that the ticket's instructions or the project's instructions forbid.
4. Check the file attachments the draft names: each one exists on the ticket, and the one the draft uses is the one the human attached.

A pass goes on with a note of what was checked. A failure goes back to the builder with each place that is wrong and why; a second return asks the human (`ask_decision`: keep going or drop) and stops there.
